Does recording survive?

This deliberately never stops the recorder itself — not when the page is hidden, not when the screen locks. Anything that stops is iOS doing it, which is the only thing worth measuring. Chunks are timestamped as they arrive, so a suspension shows up as a hole.

  1. Tap Start, then talk or play something continuously the whole time.
  2. Stay on this page for about 15 seconds.
  3. Switch to another app for about 20 seconds, then come back.
  4. Wait about 10 seconds.
  5. Lock the screen for about 20 seconds, then unlock and return.
  6. Wait about 10 seconds, then tap Stop.
